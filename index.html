Why This Corridor

Built around one real gap

Pearson VUE, Prometric, and PMI do not operate exam centres inside Iran. Oman does — and it's a short flight away.

Core Insight

Sanctioned exam networks skip Iran entirely

PMP, CCNA, CompTIA, Microsoft, CFA, and CMA all run on Pearson VUE or Prometric — neither operates in Iran. Muscat is the nearest accessible test network, making the corridor a necessity, not a convenience.

Not IELTS

Skip what's already local

IELTS is already widely available in Tehran. We focus marketing on certifications students genuinely cannot get at home.

Full Bundle

Flights + hotel + transfer + tour

One price, one itinerary — course, travel, and a Muscat city tour, so students never coordinate logistics themselves.

Trusted Verification

Certificates employers can check

MOHERI, PMI, and NEBOSH-issued certificates carry an online verification number — a stronger trust signal than local-only credentials.

Payment Model

Two pockets, zero conversion

Toman is collected in Iran. OMR is paid out in Oman. No transfer, no FX exchange — just clean reconciliation.

Toman PocketCollected by Mehdi in Tehran from students
⇄
OMR PocketPaid out by Morteza to institutes & vendors in Muscat

A notional exchange rate is used only internally to reconcile Mehdi and Morteza's profit split — no real currency ever crosses the border.
